# Film Brief Press

A one-page brief builder for [**animation-pipeline**](https://github.com/bishpls/animation-pipeline) by **Michael Bishop** ([@bishpls](https://github.com/bishpls)).

His pipeline makes films with Claude Code: songs, animation, typography and sound, all authored in code. Its `make-film` skill runs a film through six stages (frame, sound, storyboard, look, build and review, exports). This page walks you through those stages as a form and turns your answers into a brief you paste into Claude Code, running inside your clone of his repo.

## Use it

1. Open `index.html` in a browser.
2. Load an example or clear the form, then fill in the stages. Blank fields become questions Claude asks you first.
3. Click **Copy brief** and paste it into Claude Code, opened in your clone of [bishpls/animation-pipeline](https://github.com/bishpls/animation-pipeline).

Your draft is saved in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent anywhere.
